Your little painting room

Let your fingers work together.

  1. Steer. Drag one finger anywhere on the canvas. The ring moves with you, never under you.
  2. Make a mark. Keep the first finger down and add a second. Lift the second to stop.
  3. Change your line. Slide the second finger up for a wider brush, down for a finer one.

Lifting the steering finger or leaving the app raises the brush. Lift all fingers before starting again. Extra fingers are ignored. Press and hold a colour to mix your own; Reset brings back the original. Mouse: drag to paint, wheel to resize, right-click a colour to mix. Keyboard: arrows steer, hold Space to paint, + / − resize.

Keep it close

In iPhone Safari, open the Share menu (or More, then Share), choose Add to Home Screen, turn on Open as Web App if shown, then tap Add.

Open the installed app online once and wait for “Offline ready” here. Safari and the installed app may keep separate drawings. This device stores your sketch; clearing website data or storage eviction can remove it. Save a backup for keeps.
