      Your little painting room

      Let your fingers work together.

      1. Steer. Drag one finger anywhere on the canvas. The ring moves with you, never under you.
      2. Make a mark. Keep the first finger down and add a second. Lift the second to stop.
      3. Change your line. Slide the second finger up for a wider brush, down for a finer one. Small drift is ignored. Tap the size number at the bottom to set it exactly, or lock it so it never changes by accident.
      4. Reposition. Ran out of room? Lift the steering finger and put it down somewhere else, like picking up a mouse. Keep the second finger down and the line carries on.
      5. Zoom. Put two fingers down together and hold: pinch to zoom, drag to move around. Pinch all the way out to see the whole page.
      6. Undo and redo. Tap two fingers to undo, three to redo.

      Lifting the painting finger or leaving the app raises the brush. The eraser sits beside the inks. Press and hold an ink to mix your own, or pick one from the drawing; Reset brings back the original. Layers can be hidden, locked, faded and blended; a locked or hidden layer cannot be painted. Mouse: drag to paint, wheel to resize, Ctrl+wheel or trackpad pinch to zoom, right-click an ink to mix. Keyboard: arrows steer, hold Space to paint, + / − resize, ⌘Z / ⇧⌘Z undo and redo.

      Keep it close

      In iPhone Safari, open the Share menu (or More, then Share), choose Add to Home Screen, turn on Open as Web App if shown, then tap Add.

      Open the installed app online once and wait for “Offline ready” here. Safari and the installed app keep separate libraries. Drawings are stored on this device only, and iOS can clear website storage; use Save to Photos for pictures and Back up all drawings for editable copies.
