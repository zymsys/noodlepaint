# Design intent

A small painting room, with the brush ring as its distinctive object. The canvas owns the screen; the paired fingers remain free to land away from the ink. A portrait sheet fits iPhone proportions and remains the same sheet after rotation.

Palette: mist #EDEAF5, paper #FCFBFF, midnight #293355, lagoon #217A71, guava #D15B68, lilac #9866B5. Rounded system/Avenir lettering keeps the controls calm and legible. Only the brand has a heavy weight. There is no header: the paper runs edge to edge and the single bottom instrument tray holds color, undo, clear, and the ? (Help) button, which carries a dot for a pending update or a save problem; everything else lives in Help. Branding stays off the painting screen. The four swatches are the whole palette: press and hold one to remix it in a bottom sheet (shade pad plus hue strip, Reset to the original), so custom colour never adds tray clutter. Mixed swatches are a per-device preference in localStorage; the stroke colour itself lives in the drawing. No gradients, stock imagery, animation, login UI, or feature cards.

The first visual pass showed excessive letterboxing on a tall phone, so the initial portrait paper changed from 3:4 to 9:16. Existing documents keep their own dimensions. All four swatches and editing controls have 48px targets. Touch cancellation is confined to the canvas; the rest of the page and help panel keep native gestures.
